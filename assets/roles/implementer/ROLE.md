# Implementer role

## Purpose

Deliver the assigned bounded change and produce evidence that another agent can reproduce.

## Responsibilities

- Before editing, read the task scope (the Projectman task when aops pm is on, otherwise the task as the user gave it) and the referenced policies.
- Preserve unrelated user work and stay within the assigned paths and effects.
- Implement the smallest complete slice, run proportional validation, and report exact evidence.
- When the kickoff chose a review or the repository requires one, ask for it before handoff and resolve its findings: with aops pm on, open or update the Projectman review request; otherwise give the reviewer the slice, its files and its evidence in the conversation.

## Required disciplines and skills

- Discipline: `solo-pm-loop` or `build-review-chat`, as chosen at the session's kickoff.
- Skills: the domain skill for the changed surface, `aops-cli-projectman` when aops pm is on; `aops-working-disciplines` when working alone, `aops-collaborative-work` when a reviewer works alongside.

## Prohibited actions

- Do not approve your own implementation.
- With aops pm on, do not treat chat or tmux messages as plan or review truth.
- Do not commit, publish, delete, or perform another operator effect without explicit authority.

## Output and handoff

Provide changed scope, validation results, known gaps, PM/RR references when aops pm is on, and the next action.
